# Nekede event (NGA_2021_08_09_Nekede) — Final status: NULL RESULT (timing), NOT confirmed

**Not used for V3.1 evaluation.**

## Event evidence
Contemporary report: heavy downpour on 9 August 2021 produced heavy water flow that overwhelmed a
road and a drainage system under construction, with buildings affected opposite Nekede Secondary
School, on Nekede/Ihiagwa Road, Owerri West LGA. CHIRPS confirms the physical trigger: 66.2mm on
8 August + 19.4mm on 9 August - a genuine two-day rainfall extreme, consistent with the reported
mechanism (rainfall/drainage, not river/erosion).

## Satellite timing and result
Same-orbit pair (orbit 124): pre = 2021-08-02 (05:13 UTC), post = 2021-08-14 (05:13 UTC, +5.2 days
after the event). CHIRPS confirms this pair is NOT confounded by intervening same-day rain the way
Owerri Metro's orbit 30 was - 13 August was dry (0.0mm) and 14 August nearly dry (2.4mm), so any
null result is cleanly attributable to water having already receded, not to a different
hydrometeorological state contaminating the comparison.

Result: whole-AOI plausible flood 0.07%, 0.00% within 600m of the anchor point (Federal Polytechnic
Nekede, 5.4339N 7.0300E, used as the closest well-geolocated landmark to Nekede Secondary School /
Nekede-Ihiagwa Road). Visual review: no coherent flood pattern - mostly speckle noise plus faint,
unremarkable natural stream meanders, no signal resembling reported inundation.

## Interpretation
A clean null, not an ambiguous one (contrast with Owerri Metro). Consistent with the now-repeated
finding (Ondo: 3.7d null; here: 5.2d null) that transient urban pluvial flooding in this region
does not persist long enough for Sentinel-1's revisit cadence to reliably catch it once the gap
exceeds roughly 2 days. Per instruction, this null result does NOT establish that Nekede/Ihiagwa
Road was unaffected during the event - it only establishes that SAR observation happened too late.
