# Risk Assessment Method

## Objective

LandCheck evaluates information-security risks by considering:

- likelihood
- impact
- current control strength
- residual risk

## Suggested Scales

- Likelihood: Low / Medium / High
- Impact: Low / Medium / High
- Risk rating:
  - Low
  - Medium
  - High
  - Critical

## Assessment Inputs

- system changes
- incident history
- vendor dependency
- payment exposure
- public data exposure risk
- field-device and offline-sync risk

## Treatment Decisions

- Mitigate
- Accept
- Transfer
- Avoid
