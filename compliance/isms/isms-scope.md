# ISMS Scope Draft

## Scope Statement

The LandCheck Information Security Management System covers the design, development, operation, and support of the LandCheck platform used for:

- field data collection
- tree and plot management
- sponsor payments and sponsor reporting
- CSR implementation monitoring
- public impact transparency pages
- payout and evidence workflows

## In-Scope Components

- LandCheck API
- LandCheck Work
- LandCheck Green web and mobile experiences
- production database and object storage
- payment integrations used by sponsor flows
- reporting exports and operational evidence records

## Exclusions

Document any exclusions explicitly here before certification.

## Interested Parties

- LandCheck leadership
- partner organizations
- CSR clients
- public sponsors
- field agents
- payment and infrastructure providers
