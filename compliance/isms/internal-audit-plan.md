# Internal Audit Plan Template

## Objective

Assess whether LandCheck security and operational controls are designed and operating as intended.

## Audit Areas

- authentication and privileged access
- payment and payout controls
- sponsor evidence integrity
- change management
- backup and restore
- vendor oversight
- logging and incident management

## Minimum Audit Questions

1. Are privileged users approved and reviewed?
2. Are logins protected and traceable?
3. Are production changes documented and approved?
4. Are backups successful and tested?
5. Are payout anomalies reviewed?
6. Are incidents logged and followed to closure?

## Output

- findings
- severity
- owner
- due date
- closure evidence
