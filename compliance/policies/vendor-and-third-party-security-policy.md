# Vendor and Third-Party Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how LandCheck tracks and reviews third-party service providers.

## Policy Statements

1. All material service providers must be listed in the vendor register.
2. Security-relevant vendors must have an assigned owner.
3. Vendor changes, outages, or material incidents must be recorded.
4. Payment, hosting, storage, and identity-related vendors require higher scrutiny.

## Covered Vendor Types

- hosting
- storage
- payment processor
- email provider
- source code platform
- maps/geospatial provider
- analytics or AI provider
- mobile build/distribution provider
