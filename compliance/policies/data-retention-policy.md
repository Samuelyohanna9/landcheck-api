# Data Retention Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how LandCheck retains and deletes operational, financial, and security records.

## Data Categories

- authentication sessions
- activity logs
- payout records
- sponsor records
- payment records
- field evidence photos
- QR activity
- generated reports
- support and complaint records

## Policy Statements

1. Data must be retained only as long as needed for legal, operational, contractual, or security purposes.
2. Security logs must have defined retention periods.
3. Payout and financial records must be retained according to accounting and legal requirements.
4. Public-impact content must not reveal private data beyond approved disclosure scope.
5. Deletion or purge operations must be controlled and logged where applicable.

## Implementation Notes

- Application session and activity-log retention should align with the production env settings.
- Manual retention decisions must be documented if they differ by customer contract or regulatory need.
