# Change Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how LandCheck plans, approves, deploys, and reviews changes to production systems.

## Policy Statements

1. Production changes must be documented before deployment.
2. Material changes must identify:
  - business reason
  - technical scope
  - approver
  - rollback plan
  - validation steps
3. Emergency changes must be documented retrospectively within one business day.
4. Security-sensitive changes must be reviewed before release where practical.
5. Deployment evidence must be retained.

## Change Types

- standard
- normal
- emergency
